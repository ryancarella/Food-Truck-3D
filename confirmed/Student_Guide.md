# Carellas Kitchen · Venture 3D Studio

Open **https://ryancarella.github.io/Food-Truck-3D/**.

1. **Explore:** drag the truck to orbit, scroll to zoom, right drag to pan. Use camera buttons and Fit to return to a clear view.
2. **Edit:** select a part on the truck or in Scene. Change position, rotation, or scale in the right inspector. The toolbar offers visual move, rotate, and resize handles. Dimensions use approximate meters.
3. **Style:** choose a color and material finish. Expand Branding & texture to upload an image, edit a sign, or adjust texture repeat and placement.
4. **Save:** click Save project for this browser. Download editable backup to keep a portable copy. Open project / GLB restores it. Keep JSON for component editing; imported GLBs preserve finished geometry and materials.
5. **Confirm:** click Confirm model, wait for validation, then click Download confirmed handoff ZIP. It includes the GLB, manifest, editable project, preview, and instructions.

**Mission 3:** carry `Venture_3D_Confirmed_Handoff.zip` forward. Extract its files together; use `model.glb` and `model-manifest.json` for website integration. Keep `project.json` to continue editing.

The truck is an editable stylized interpretation of your concept, with approximate proportions. It is not measured CAD or a print-ready mesh. Browser saves can be cleared, so download your work.
